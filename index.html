About the Venue & Event

Welcome to the Youth Innovations Marketplace at the Diplomatic Main Hall, Manila Marriott Hotel. This marketplace brings together 20 youth-led satellite innovation teams solving real-world challenges across climate tech, community health, education, and digital inclusion.

🎪 20 Innovation Booths

Two double-sided booth rows flank the center aisle. Booths 01–05 and 11–15 face the center aisle; walk around to the outer aisles for 06–10 and 16–20.

📺 Booth TVs

Every booth has a TV looping its video silently. Click or tap a TV (or press E while looking at it) to open it in a pop-up with sound.

🏛️ Organizer Booths & Mascots

Terre des hommes, Kindernothilfe and Violence Ends With Us (VEWU) have their stands along the east side of the Pre-Function Foyer. Say hello to Tutu the Tdh elephant and the VEWU lighthouse on the way.

🎤 Stage & Keynote Screen

On the North wall with continuous live presentations. Take a VIP seat to watch with full audio.

📝 Commitment Wall

In the Pre-Function Foyer, across from this desk. Post your pledge for youth leadership and sustainability — it's pinned on the 3D wall for every visitor in the hall.

🌟 Digital Passport

Visit all 20 exhibits and pass their quizzes to complete your passport. Tap a booth on the radar to track it.
