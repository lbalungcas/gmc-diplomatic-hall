# Registration Team Guide: Youth Innovations Marketplace check-in

**17 November 2026 · Diplomatic Hall, Marriott Hotel Manila · Registration desk opens 9:00 AM**

## The 5 steps at the desk

1. **Greet** the guest and ask for their QR code (on their phone or printed).
2. **Scan** it with the scanner page.
3. **Read the screen**: the guest's name, and **📷 Photos OK** or **🚫 NO PHOTOS**.
4. **Hand over the reg kit**, then tap **Kit given** at the bottom of the scanner.
5. If the screen said **NO PHOTOS**, apply the no-photo marker (lanyard colour or badge sticker, as agreed with the media team).

No QR code? See **Protocol 1** (walk-ins) and **Protocol 2** (registered, but no QR) below.

## Before doors open (by 8:30 AM)

1. Charge your phone fully, and bring a power bank if you have one.
2. On your phone, open **https://youthinnovationsmarketplace.org/register/admin/login**.
3. Enter the email and password the coordinator gave you, then tap **Sign in**. Do this the day before if you can: you stay signed in on that phone.
4. You'll land on the scanner. Tap **Start scanning** and **allow camera access** when asked.
5. Turn the sound on and the volume up. Each result has its own beep.
6. Test it: scan your own test ticket, or ask the coordinator for one.
7. Make sure the **walk-in QR code** (the sign that links to the registration form) is printed and placed at the desk.

On an iPhone, use **Safari**. On Android, use **Chrome**.

## Scanning

Hold the guest's QR code inside the frame. The result fills the screen for about 2 seconds, then the scanner is ready again. Tap the screen to dismiss a result early.

| Screen | Sound | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **CHECKED IN ✓** | High double beep | Approved, first arrival | Welcome them in. Give the reg kit and tap **Kit given**. Check the photo badge. |
| 🟡 **ALREADY CHECKED IN !** | Two medium beeps | This ticket was already used, at the time shown | Ask if they stepped out and are coming back. If it looks like someone else is using their ticket, send them to the **help desk**. |
| 🔴 **NOT APPROVED ✕** | Low buzz | Registration is *pending*, *waitlisted* or *declined* (shown on screen) | **Do not admit yet.** Send them to the **help desk** to be confirmed. |
| 🔴 **INVALID CODE ✕** | Low buzz | Not one of our tickets | Ask for the QR from their "You're confirmed" email. If they don't have it, follow **Protocol 2**. |
| ⬛ **NO CONNECTION** | Low buzz | The phone couldn't reach the server | Scan again. If it keeps happening, use manual check-in (below) or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. |

The numbers at the top show how many people are checked in (out of everyone approved) and how many reg kits have been given.

## Reg kit ("Received Reg Kit?")

- Give a reg kit **only after a successful check-in**.
- After you hand it over, tap **Kit given**. It turns green and shows the time.
  - **On the scanner:** the last guest you scanned stays in a bar at the bottom of the screen, with the **Kit given** button. It stays there until you scan the next person.
  - **In Search (manual check-in):** every checked-in guest has a **Kit given** button next to **Undo**.
- Tapped it by mistake? Tap it again and confirm to untick it.
- If a guest comes back saying they didn't get a kit, look them up in **Search**: the button shows whether a kit was recorded and when.
- Admins see a **Reg kit** column in the dashboard and can export it to the attendance sheet (CSV columns **Received Reg Kit?** and **Reg kit given at**).

## Protocol 1: walk-in (did not sign up online)

Walk-ins still register through the online form, so they get their QR code and the safeguarding form by email.

1. **Point them to the walk-in QR code** at the desk. They scan it with their phone and fill in the registration form. It takes a few minutes.
   - **Under 18?** The form asks for their parent or guardian's name and email, and the guardian's consent. They need this to register.
   - **No phone, or no data?** An admin can fill in the form with them on a desk phone or tablet. Use the guest's own email address, because that is where the QR code and safeguarding form are sent.
2. Once they've submitted the form, **send them to the help desk**.
3. **Help desk (admin):** open the dashboard, tick **Registered today**, and set **Status: Pending**. Find the guest, check their photo ID, open their record and tap **Approve**.
   - They immediately get the confirmation email with their **QR code** and the **Child Safeguarding and Code of Conduct form** link.
4. Ask them to **complete the safeguarding form** (the link is in that email).
5. **Check them in**: scan the QR from their email, or tap **Check in now** in their record.
6. Give the reg kit and tap **Kit given**.

## Protocol 2: signed up online, but no QR code

1. Ask them to open the **"Open my ticket"** link in their confirmation email. The ticket page shows their QR. Scan it.
2. Can't find the email? Tap **Search** (top right of the scanner) and type their name or organization (at least 2 letters).
   - Confirm their name, organization and category with them, and **check photo ID**.
3. What you see in Search:
   - **Approved:** tap **Check in**, give the reg kit and tap **Kit given**.
   - **Pending** or **Waitlisted:** send them to the **help desk**. The admin can approve them on the spot; that also emails their QR code and the safeguarding form.
   - **Declined:** send them to the help desk. Don't admit them.
4. **Not found?** Try other spellings, their preferred name, or their organization. If they still aren't there, treat them as a **walk-in** (Protocol 1).

## Other QR problems

1. Ask them to **turn the screen brightness up**, zoom in, or hold the phone steady about 20 cm away.
2. If they have a ticket link or code, type or paste it into the box at the bottom of the scanner and tap **Check**.
3. If none of that works, use **Search** as in Protocol 2.

## Photo consent

Every check-in shows one of:

- **📷 Photos OK.** The guest agreed to be photographed or filmed.
- **🚫 NO PHOTOS.** The guest did **not** agree. Follow the media team's process for marking them, e.g. a lanyard colour or badge sticker.

This matters a lot here. Some guests are children, and this is a child-protection event.

## Mistakes

- **Checked in the wrong person:** open **Search**, find them, and tap **Undo**. This also clears their reg kit tick.
- **Ticked Kit given by mistake:** tap the green button again and confirm.

Every check-in, undo and kit tick is logged with your name.

## Help desk (admins only)

Only admins can approve people, see contact details or fix a registration. Send these to the help desk:

- walk-ins who have submitted the form (Protocol 1)
- pending, waitlisted or declined guests
- spelling corrections
- anything you're unsure about

When an admin approves someone, the guest's QR code works straight away, and they get the confirmation email with the QR and the safeguarding form. Admins can also resend that email, or send the safeguarding form again, from the guest's record.

## Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Camera permission was denied" | iPhone: Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow, then reload. Android: tap the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions → Camera → Allow, then reload. |
| Camera is black | Close other apps using the camera, then reload the page. |
| Sent back to the sign-in page | Sign in again with your email and password. Forgot it? Tap **Forgot password?**, or ask the coordinator. |
| Page says you don't have access | Ask the coordinator to add your email as a scanner. |
| "Check the guest in before giving the reg kit" | The guest isn't checked in. Check them in first, then tap **Kit given**. |
| Walk-in's form says registration is closed | Tell the help desk admin. They can extend **Registration closes** in **Settings**. |
| Phone dying | Switch to a backup phone, or check people in by name from a laptop at the desk. |
