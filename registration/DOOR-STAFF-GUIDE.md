# Door Staff Guide: Youth Innovations Marketplace check-in

**17 November 2026 · Diplomatic Hall, Marriott Hotel Manila · Registration desk opens 9:00 AM**

## Before doors open (by 8:30 AM)

1. Charge your phone fully, and bring a power bank if you have one.
2. On your phone, open **https://youthinnovationsmarketplace.org/register/admin/login**.
3. Enter the email and password the coordinator gave you, then tap **Sign in**. Do this the day before if you can: you stay signed in on that phone.
4. You'll land on the scanner. Tap **Start scanning** and **allow camera access** when asked.
5. Turn the sound on and the volume up. Each result has its own beep.
6. Test it: scan your own test ticket, or ask the coordinator for one.

On an iPhone, use **Safari**. On Android, use **Chrome**.

## Scanning

Hold the guest's QR code (phone screen or printout) inside the frame. The result fills the screen for about 2 seconds, then the scanner is ready again. Tap the screen to dismiss a result early.

| Screen | Sound | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **CHECKED IN ✓** | High double beep | Approved, first arrival | Welcome them in. Check the photo badge (below). |
| 🟡 **ALREADY CHECKED IN !** | Two medium beeps | This ticket was already used, at the time shown | Ask if they stepped out and are coming back. If it looks like someone else is using their ticket, send them to the **help desk**. |
| 🔴 **NOT APPROVED ✕** | Low buzz | Registration is *pending*, *waitlisted* or *declined* (shown on screen) | **Do not admit.** Send them to the **help desk**. |
| 🔴 **INVALID CODE ✕** | Low buzz | Not one of our tickets | Ask for the QR from their "You're confirmed" email. If they don't have it, send them to the **help desk**. |
| ⬛ **NO CONNECTION** | Low buzz | The phone couldn't reach the server | Scan again. If it keeps happening, use manual check-in (below) or switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data. |

The number at the top (e.g. **142 / 200**) is people checked in out of everyone approved.

## Photo consent

Every check-in shows one of:

- **📷 Photos OK.** The guest agreed to be photographed or filmed.
- **🚫 NO PHOTOS.** The guest did **not** agree. Follow the media team's process for marking them, e.g. a lanyard colour or badge sticker.

This matters a lot here. Some guests are children, and this is a child-protection event.

## When the QR won't scan

1. Ask them to **turn the screen brightness up**, zoom in, or hold the phone steady about 20 cm away.
2. Ask them to open the **"Open my ticket"** link in their email. The ticket page shows a fresh QR.
3. If they have a ticket link or code, type or paste it into the box at the bottom of the scanner and tap **Check**.
4. If none of that works, use **manual check-in**:
   - Tap **Search** (top right).
   - Type their name or organization, at least 2 letters.
   - Check the name, organization and category with the guest. Ask for photo ID if unsure.
   - Tap **Check in**. If it says **Not approved**, don't admit them; send them to the help desk.

## Mistakes

If you checked in the wrong person, open **Search**, find them, and tap **Undo**. Every check-in and undo is logged with your name.

## Help desk (admins only)

Only admins can approve people, see contact details or fix a registration. Send these to the help desk:

- walk-ins without a registration
- pending, waitlisted or declined guests
- spelling corrections
- anything you're unsure about

Admins can approve someone on the spot. The guest's QR code then works straight away, and they also get a confirmation email.

## Problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| "Camera permission was denied" | iPhone: Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow, then reload. Android: tap the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions → Camera → Allow, then reload. |
| Camera is black | Close other apps using the camera, then reload the page. |
| Sent back to the sign-in page | Sign in again with your email and password. Forgot it? Tap **Forgot password?**, or ask the coordinator. |
| Page says you don't have access | Ask the coordinator to add your email as a scanner. |
| Phone dying | Switch to a backup phone, or check people in by name from a laptop at the desk. |
